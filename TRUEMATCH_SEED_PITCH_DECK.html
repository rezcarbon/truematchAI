Six Non-Bypassable Compliance Gates

Every assessment passes all 6 gates or verdict is blocked:

Gate 1: Coherence Check
All signals (keyword, semantic, evidence) must align within acceptable range
Gate 2: Temporal Depth Processor
Timeline must be physically possible (can't claim 10 years at 25 years old)
Gate 3: Lipschitz Constraint
Confidence bounded by evidence density (high claim = more evidence required)
Gate 4: Physiological Evidence Integrator
All evidence sources verified (GitHub commits verified, DOI papers checked, patents confirmed)
Gate 5: Pre-Linguistic Thought Separator
Inferences flagged separately from factual claims
Gate 6: Gate Orchestrator
Single failure = entire verdict blocked (non-bypassable enforcement)

Result: EU AI Act Article 12-15 compliant + NYC Local Law 144 audit-ready + GDPR Article 22 aligned

How Compliance Gates Work Together

Real example: Candidate claims "Lead engineer at Amazon for 15 years"

Gate 1 (Coherence): LinkedIn shows 3 years at Amazon (not 15) → flag

Gate 2 (Temporal): Started at Amazon age 22, can't have 15 years → flag

Gate 3 (Lipschitz): Claim high confidence, but GitHub shows junior-level work → flag

Gate 4 (Evidence): DOI papers don't match claimed expertise → flag

Gate 5 (Logic): All evidence contradicts resume → inferences marked as such

Gate 6 (Orchestrator): 5 flags = verdict blocked. Assessment cannot proceed.

Result: Candidate either corrects resume or doesn't pass assessment. No guessing.
