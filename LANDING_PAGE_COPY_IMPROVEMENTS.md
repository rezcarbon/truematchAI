## 🎨 Tone & Voice Guidelines

### DO
- **Be specific**: Numbers, evidence, outcomes
- **Be honest**: "Guessing" vs "knows", "caught" vs "detected"
- **Be direct**: Short sentences, active voice
- **Be empowering**: Focus on what they gain, not what they avoid
- **Be evidence-first**: Always back up claims with data or mechanism

### DON'T
- Generic corporate language ("leverage", "synergy", "paradigm")
- Vague promises ("best-in-class", "industry-leading", "cutting-edge")
- Passive voice ("was found that...")
- Jargon without explanation
- Features without outcomes
