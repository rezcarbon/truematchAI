PART 9: RISKS + MITIGATION
================================================================================

### RISK #1: HIRING BEFORE SEED CLOSES
**Risk:** Extend offers to candidates but seed doesn't close; can't pay salaries

Mitigation:
  - Only extend offers AFTER seed term sheet signed
  - Include contingency in offer (subject to seed closing)
  - Have backup plan (offer delayed start if seed delayed)
  - Manage candidate expectations clearly

---

### RISK #2: WRONG HIRE TAKES EQUITY
**Risk:** Hire someone who doesn't work out; they have 2.5% dilution forever

Mitigation:
  - Extend equity only after 6-month cliff
  - Use vesting schedule (4-year vest, 1-year cliff standard)
  - Reserve larger equity pool for future hires (option pool)
  - Clear performance expectations upfront

---

### RISK #3: CANDIDATE TURNS DOWN OFFER
**Risk:** Top candidates choose other startups; you miss hiring window

Mitigation:
  - Have backup candidates (top 5-10 per role)
  - Move fast (offer within 1-2 weeks of interviews)
  - De-risk mission/market uncertainty (show product, traction, capital)
  - Competitive comp + equity + patent equity helps

---

### RISK #4: DILUTION TOO HIGH FOR INVESTORS
**Risk:** Investors see you diluted to 5%, want lower valuation

Mitigation:
  - Educate investors on hired founders (expected in strong companies)
  - Show that new hires enable faster execution (worth the dilution)
  - Reference: Strong startups have lean early caps (better to be 46% of $100M than 50% of $10M)
  - Typical: Founders go from 100% → 50-60% by Series B (normal)

---

### RISK #5: LINKEDIN POST ATTRACTS WRONG PEOPLE
**Risk:** Low-quality candidates waste time; you get spammed

Mitigation:
  - Clearly filter: "5+ years enterprise sales" (not just "sales experience")
  - Screening call gate (30 min call before longer interviews)
  - Rez does first screen (saves time for both sides)
  - Polite rejection template (professional courtesy)
